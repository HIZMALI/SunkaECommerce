# Sun-Ka / Larton brand audit

Research date: 13 November 2026. The three requested sites were reachable by HTTP. This audit uses official site HTML, CSS and locally inspected official photography; it does not infer product certifications.

## Brand identity and visual direction

- [Sunka Paper](https://www.sunkapaper.com/) currently uses a combined **sun·ka / larton** header lockup: green SUN-KA, navy LARTON and a document/arrow emblem. The actual header PNG is saved unchanged as `/images/logo.png`. The alternate official lockup is `/images/logo-light.png`; an older logo remains `/images/logo-dark.png`. The demo should use the current combined lockup, rather than inventing a new logo or asserting an unverified legal group structure.
- Official CSS uses **#003761 navy**, **#3d3d3d charcoal**, **#f5f1eb stone** and **#6f983f green**. Product photographs add gold, silver, kraft and grey. The demo can use warm paper backgrounds, navy typography, restrained recycled-paper green and material gold accents.
- The official sites share Sun-Ka/Larton naming, recycled-paper manufacturing and product categories. [Larton Turkey](https://www.larton.com.tr/) supplies supporting Turkish context, including over 30 years of experience, 100% recycled paper and made-to-order sizes, shapes and textures. Do not turn these corporate statements into universal food-contact or recyclability certification for each finished product.

## Corporate and sustainability claims

[Corporate](https://www.sunkapaper.com/corporate) states over 30 years of experience, 100% recycled raw materials and customization in size, thickness and colour. The homepage describes packaging/logistics, food/retail, and manufacturing/furniture use cases; closed-loop recovery; paper separation, cleaning and fibre treatment; and waste reduction. These should be attributed at company/process level. The official production/export statistics were deliberately not promoted to live commercial KPIs.

## Project Gamma reference analysis

[Project Gamma](https://shopprojectgamma.com/) was inspected for interaction and hierarchy only: bold product-led storefront, vehicle/brand discovery, category navigation, white product-card stages, swatches, quick view, search and slide-out cart. Transfer these principles to application → category → size/shape → finish discovery, wholesale packs and RFQ. Its brand assets, photography, code, wording, reviews, promotions, shipping promises and payment logos are not used. This demo must express Sun-Ka industrial materials rather than duplicate an automotive store.
