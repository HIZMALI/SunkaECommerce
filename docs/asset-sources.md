# Official image sources

Retrieved 13 November 2026 from the official Sunka Paper and Larton websites. All source URLs returned decodable images. Photography is used for this local customer brand demonstration under the user’s instruction; public distribution and commercial rights still require company confirmation. No Project Gamma assets, unlicensed third-party stock or generated product photos are used.

## Processing and interpretation

- Product images were resized proportionally to a maximum 1600 px and saved as WebP quality 86; original PNG logos retain their original pixels and transparency. A few already optimized official web images are 833 × 677.
- The actual combined official header logo is `logo.png` (199 × 103). `logo-light.png` is the alternate official version. `logo-dark.png` is an older Sunka-only lockup.
- Gallery photos show the material family; photos do not certify that a demo SKU, finish, exact sample size, quantity or food-contact suitability is available.
- `mono-square.webp` is a stable local filename only: the inspected photo shows a **black rectangular tabbed board**, not a square board. Product copy must follow the image and sample variant configuration.
- `salmon-rectangle.webp` shows a gold square presentation; `salmon.webp` shows the rectangular presentation.
- Small official sustainability thumbnails have text baked into the artwork and are not suitable as large editorial backgrounds. `factory.webp` and `factory-exterior.webp` are genuine aerial photos linked from the official Sunka corporate page (640 × 221), not evidence of particular machinery or capacity.
- `factory-banner.webp` is a recycling foliage illustration published on Larton; the filename is a source-download convenience, not a claim that it depicts a factory.
- Source site HTML snapshots are kept in `docs/research/` for provenance only. No source application code was reused.
