# Customer presentation · 5–7 minutes

Open **http://127.0.0.1:3000**. Use a clean browser context. All businesses entered during the demo should be fictional. Tell the audience that official product imagery is real and commercial configurations/prices are representative.

## 0:00–1:00 · Discover the material story

Start on the homepage: actual Sun-Ka/Larton lockup, presentation-board photograph, product families and material responsibility. Open **Products** to show the mega menu. Switch **EN / TR** briefly, then return to EN. On a phone, use the hamburger menu and filter drawer.

## 1:00–2:00 · Find the right board

In **The Board Finder**, select **Food & Retail → Cake & Salmon Boards → Round → Metalised → Find my board**. The results are real seeded catalogue items and the URL includes the choices. Reset filters, try a color/application filter or search `SK-SAMPLE-001`. Open **Quick view**, close it with Escape, then open **Die Cut Round Boards**.

## 2:00–3:15 · Configure and order

Use **Explore Buyer Demo** or sign in with `buyer@sunka.demo` / `BuyerDemo!2026`. Show sample format, shape, surface/color and pack controls. Select the 300 mm format; compare 100 / 500 / 1000 piece tiers. Explain the DEMO/USD label and unconfirmed freight/tax. Add to the cart; add another variant if useful. Open cart, change quantity and refresh to demonstrate persistence.

Continue to **Demo checkout**. Seed buyer details are fictional and prefilled. Show **Purchase Order (Demo)**, with no card field. Press **Record demo order**. The success page has a unique order ID; no charge or email occurs. Open **Account**, locate the order and **Start a reorder** to restore variant/quantity selections.

## 3:15–4:15 · Request a custom specification

Open **Custom Solutions / Request a custom quote**. Choose a cornerboard family, provide application, `55 × 55 × 1100 mm`, estimated quantity and fictional contact details. Record the RFQ. Show its unique ID and its entry under **Account → Quote requests**. Explain that no file is uploaded and no commercial offer or email is automatically issued.

## 4:15–5:45 · Run the business view

Open `/login` and sign in as `admin@sunka.demo` / `SunkaDemo!2026`. Dashboard numbers are labelled sample data. Open **Orders** and update a status; expand a row for the saved line configurations and addresses. Open **Quote requests**, find the new RFQ, set **IN_REVIEW**, write an explicitly demo response and save. Refresh to show persistence. Under **Product catalogue**, edit a sample price or visibility and save; show the storefront update. Restore the sample price when finished.

## 5:45–6:45 · Approval and responsibility

In a separate browser context, sign in as `pending@sunka.demo` / `PendingDemo!2026`. Prices are unavailable. In the administrator context, approve **Paper & Co. · Sample Company** under wholesale applications. Reload the pending buyer product page to reveal sample pricing. Return the application to **PENDING** after the presentation if this scenario will be shown again.

Close on **Sustainability**, **Industries** or the custom quotation CTA. Name the next decisions: real catalogue/specification, price/MOQ, payment/ERP, freight/tax and approved policies. The demo is ready to explore; live trading requires those inputs and integrations.
